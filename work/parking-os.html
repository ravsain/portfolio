Product work & implementation

  1. Built a multi-society platform for gate entry and exit, visitor passes, vehicle search and alerts.
  2. Provided distinct workflows for administrators, security guards and residents.
  3. Tracked live parking occupancy with Redis counters, pub/sub and WebSocket updates.
  4. Added camera-based number-plate OCR to support vehicle recognition at the gate.
  5. Packaged the application with Docker for cloud deployment or self-hosting.
One operating pictureGate activity, vehicle records and parking availability are brought together for multiple residential societies.

Outcome

A deployable platform for society gate operations and parking, with real-time occupancy and vehicle recognition.
