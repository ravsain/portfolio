SkillRadar: which tech skills are worth learning next?

Capstone project · 2024

An end-to-end analytics and machine-learning platform that answers three questions: which tech skills are in demand, what they pay, and where the market is heading in 2027–28.

Role
Solo: research, data pipeline, models and dashboards

Research, data & product delivery

  1. Framed the analysis around practical questions: which skills are in demand, what they pay and how demand may change.
  2. Built a Python pipeline collecting live data from 9 public APIs across 65 technology skills.
  3. Forecast demand to 2028 with Holt-Winters models and visible 80% confidence intervals.
  4. Trained a RandomForest salary model with an R² of 0.87.
  5. Presented results through Plotly visualisations in Streamlit and Flask dashboards.
From research to decisionsThe pipeline turns market signals into forecasts that can inform skill planning, hiring and training.

Outcome

A working market-intelligence tool with forecasts and salary predictions, published on GitHub.
