SkillRadar: which tech skills are worth learning next?

Capstone project · 2024

An end-to-end analytics and machine-learning platform that answers three questions: which tech skills are in demand, what they pay, and where the market is heading in 2027–28.

Role
Solo: research, data pipeline, models and dashboards
Timeline
2024
Stack
Python, scikit-learn, statsmodels, Plotly, Streamlit, Flask
Links

Three ways to read this project

All of them are here. The one that matches the view you picked comes first.

Product lens

matches your view
  • Started from the user's question, not the data: what should I learn, what will it pay, and will it still matter in two years?
  • Two ways to explore the answers: an interactive Streamlit app and a Flask web dashboard.
  • Forecasts are shown with 80% confidence intervals, so the uncertainty is visible.

Engineering lens

matches your view
  • Python pipeline pulling live data from 9 public APIs, covering 65 tech skills.
  • Holt-Winters time-series forecasting (statsmodels) to 2028.
  • RandomForest salary predictor (scikit-learn) with R² 0.87.
  • Plotly charts served through Streamlit and Flask.

Operations lens

matches your view
  • One repeatable pipeline from data collection to dashboards.
  • Forecasts that can support planning decisions, such as which skills a team should hire or train for.

Outcome

A working market-intelligence tool with forecasts and salary predictions, published on GitHub.
