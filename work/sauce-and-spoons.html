Sauce and Spoons: piloting tabletop menu tablets

Capstone simulation · Oct 2024

A project-management capstone: plan, run and close a pilot of tabletop menu tablets for a restaurant, aimed at faster service and higher guest turnover.

Project scope & delivery

  1. Scoped a tabletop ordering pilot to address slow service and improve guest turnover.
  2. Defined SMART goals and mapped stakeholder influence to guide decisions and communication.
  3. Built a Gantt-based plan using three-point estimates for the project schedule.
  4. Identified delivery risks and established quality standards and execution benchmarks.
  5. Prepared closing and impact reports focused on revenue and customer satisfaction.
End-to-end project planThe work connects the proposed service improvement to scope, schedule, risk, quality and measurable outcomes.

Outcome

A complete, documented project lifecycle from initiation to closing, with impact reported on revenue and customer satisfaction.
