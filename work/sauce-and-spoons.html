Two ways to read this project

All of them are here. The one that matches the view you picked comes first.

Product lens

matches your view
  • Framed the problem: slow service and low guest turnover, with tabletop tablets as the proposed fix.
  • Defined scope and SMART goals so success could be measured.
  • Mapped stakeholders on a power grid to decide who to involve, and how.

Operations lens

matches your view
  • Built the plan with a Gantt structure and three-point time estimates.
  • Wrote risk management strategies grounded in industry research.
  • Set quality management standards and evaluated execution against benchmarks.
  • Delivered closing and impact reports on revenue and customer satisfaction.

Outcome

A complete, documented project lifecycle from initiation to closing, with impact reported on revenue and customer satisfaction.
