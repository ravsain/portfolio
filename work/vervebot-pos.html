Rebuilding a Point of Sale from scratch

Vervebot · Apr 2025 – present

At Vervebot I led the end-to-end redesign of the company's Point of Sale system on a Go backend, drove the integration of an in-house payment gateway, rolled out a Python LLM agent for merchants, and moved the team to Scrumban to deliver it all.

Product & Quality Analyst (Automation). Led the redesign.

A system from initial request to release, with dedicated paths for payments, merchant assistance and quality checks.

My role & contributions

  1. Led the redesign from client requirements and PRDs through backlog priorities and release coordination.
  2. Rebuilt the POS on a scalable Go backend and integrated the in-house payment gateway.
  3. Shipped a Python LLM agent for merchant sales insights and automated support answers.
  4. Introduced Scrumban with Jira and Trello, aligning business and engineering around sprint work.
  5. Added Playwright end-to-end and API automation to protect critical workflows and regression checks.
One connected deliveryProduct decisions, backend implementation, test coverage and team workflow were handled as parts of the same POS rebuild.

Outcome

A POS rebuilt on a scalable Go backend, payments running through the company's own gateway, an AI agent helping merchants, and a team delivering through Scrumban with automated regression checks.
