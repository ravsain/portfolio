Rebuilding a Point of Sale from scratch

Vervebot · Apr 2025 – present

At Vervebot I led the end-to-end redesign of the company's Point of Sale system on a Go backend, drove the integration of an in-house payment gateway, rolled out a Python LLM agent for merchants, and moved the team to Scrumban to deliver it all.

Product & Quality Analyst (Automation). Led the redesign.

Three ways to read this project

All of them are here. The one that matches the view you picked comes first.

Product lens

matches your view
  • Translated client and business requirements into technical specs and PRDs for each sprint.
  • Owned requirements and backlog priorities for the redesign, keeping business goals and engineering work aligned.
  • Shaped the payment gateway integration around the company's revenue goals.
  • Defined what the AI agent should do for merchants: real-time sales insights and automated answers to support queries.

Engineering lens

matches your view
  • Rebuilt the POS backend in Go (Golang), designed to scale, with goroutines handling concurrent API load.
  • Integrated a Python LLM agent into the Go POS for sales insights and support automation.
  • Integrated the custom in-house payment gateway.
  • Built Playwright end-to-end suites and API automation around critical user workflows.

Operations lens

matches your view
  • Moved the development team to Scrumban: daily standups, backlog grooming and task tracking in Trello and custom Jira tooling.
  • Coordinated the payment gateway rollout between business and engineering.
  • Automated regression scenarios to cut regression testing time and catch defects before release.
  • Bridged clients and engineering so every sprint started from a clear spec.

Outcome

A POS rebuilt on a scalable Go backend, payments running through the company's own gateway, an AI agent helping merchants, and a team delivering through Scrumban with automated regression checks.
